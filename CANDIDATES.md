# Boss word candidates (draft pass)

Exactly one word each, at most 30 characters, describing my
boss; judged on funniest and truest.

1. calendar -- the boss as a schedule with opinions.
2. recurring -- the boss as a meeting that cannot be
   declined, only rescheduled; the truest single word in
   the set, because it also describes the feeling: it
   comes back, weekly, at the same time, forever.
3. cc'd -- copied into everything, author of nothing.
4. quarterly -- speaks four times a year, in targets.
5. out-of-office -- present in the building, absent in the
   thread.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2, "recurring". "Calendar" describes the
boss's form; "recurring" describes the boss's physics --
the meeting that cannot be declined, only survived, and
that returns at the same time next week with the same
agenda and slightly more attendees. It is also the word for
the feeling, which is what a one-word entry has to carry.
